# Algorithms

### Smart Shuffle
---
Imagine that you enjoy creating specific playlists in your music player. Let's say you created a grunge playlist, but you're a purist and want to include songs from grunge bands that emerged in Seattle during the 80s and 90s. But you're an even bigger purist, so your playlist features only the "Big Four" of grunge.

However, when you hit shuffle, you're not satisfied with how the songs are being played. This algorithm aims to make your shuffle experience more interesting by playing one song from each artist per "round". Only after every artist has played one song will the playlist move on to the next round.
